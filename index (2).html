Trip log: Rajasthan, 5 days

Day 1Udaipur

Lakes at golden hour

Walk the old city in the morning when it is quiet. Save the lakeside for the last hour of light.

Tip: Book a boat ride for sunset, not midday. The heat is much easier.
Day 2Udaipur

City Palace and a slow lunch

Go when it opens. Afterwards, eat somewhere with a rooftop and stay longer than planned.

Day 3Kumbhalgarh

The long wall

The fort wall winds across the hills for miles. Wear good shoes and carry water.

Tip: Start before 8 am to beat the crowds and the sun.
Day 4Ranakpur

Carved marble, quiet halls

The Jain temple has over a thousand carved pillars, and no two look the same. Cover your shoulders and knees.

Day 5Home

Write it down

Before the details fade, note the best meal, the best view and the one thing you'd skip next time.
